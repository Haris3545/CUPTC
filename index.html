Questions.

Do I need to have played before?

No. Social membership is for everyone, whatever your level. Full membership is for players who want to compete, and places are decided at trials on 6 October.

Where do we play?

At the Cambridge University Sports Centre. Members get session details after joining.

What happens after I pay?

You'll get the password to the members area straight away, where the shop and member information live.

How do I get in touch?

Message us on Instagram at @camunipadelclub or email secretary@cuptc.com.
